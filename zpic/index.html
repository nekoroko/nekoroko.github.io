What it does

AF point overlay

A thin box shows where the camera focused. Press Space to zoom to 100% right on it.

Focus peaking

Three styles (Q): colored edges, glowing edges on dark mono, or a tint over the in-focus area.

Instant frame flipping

Shows the embedded JPEG instantly, then swaps in a real RAW render in the background — same look, real detail.

Zoomed flipping

Stay at 100% and press ← →: each frame opens centered on its own AF point. Ideal for bird bursts.

One-hand keys

Z keep, X reject, 1–5 stars, Ctrl/⌘+Z undo. Fewer than ten keys to learn.

Safe hand-off

Move rejects to a _rejected folder (RAW+JPEG pairs and .xmp move together). ZPic never deletes your files.

Lightroom hand-off

Star ratings are saved as you rate, in a form Lightroom reads on import: an XMP sidecar for NEF, the rating field inside the file for JPEG. Color labels are left alone.

Good to know

  • High Efficiency RAW (HE / HE★) can't be decoded, so ZPic shows the embedded JPEG instead (marked “JPEG view”). Lossless compressed NEF is fully supported.
  • JPEG-only frames work, but have no AF point.
  • Keep/reject marks are saved in a small .zpic.json file in the folder. Your NEF files are never modified. For JPEGs, only the star rating field is written; the image itself is untouched. Sidecars written by other apps are never touched.
  • ZPic never sends your photos anywhere. It goes online only once, to check your license key.
  • The free trial runs for 14 days with every feature. A license key removes the time limit, and one key works for up to 5 activations.
  • The app is in English and Japanese. It follows your system language; you can change it in Settings.
  • Free updates for all 1.x versions.

Opening it the first time

ZPic is made by a solo developer and isn't registered with Apple or Microsoft, so your computer shows a warning the first time. You only do this once.

macOS 15 Sequoia / 26 Tahoe
  1. Unzip and drag ZPic into Applications.
  2. Double-click ZPic. When it says ZPic was not opened, click Done (not “Move to Trash”).
  3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll to Security and click Open Anyway next to ZPic (available for about an hour).
  4. Enter your login password. From now on it opens normally.

Download the right version: Apple Silicon (M1 or later) or Intel.

Windows 10 / 11
  1. Right-click the ZIP › Extract All. Keep the whole ZPic folder together.
  2. Double-click ZPic.exe. On “Windows protected your PC”, click More info › Run anyway.

If Smart App Control blocks it with no “Run anyway” button, ZPic can't run on that PC — ask me for a refund.
